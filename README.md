 heres a 🚀 QUICK START TUTORIAL

Follow these simple steps to start protecting your games instantly:

### 1. Launch the App
* Download the **`SaveGuard.exe`** file from our [Releases](https://github.com) section on the right side of this page.
* Double-click it to run. It requires **zero installation** or setup.

### 2. Choose Your Game Save Folder (Source)
* Click the top **Browse** button.
* Select the exact folder where your game actively keeps its live saves. 
* *(Tip: If you aren't sure where it is, look under common Steam paths listed below!)*

### 3. Choose Your Backup Location (Destination)
* Click the second **Browse** button.
* Pick a safe folder where you want your secure backups stored (like an external drive or a dedicated "Game Backups" folder on your desktop).

### 4. Run It!
* **Manual Snapshot:** Click **🚀 Manual Backup** to take an instant snapshot before messing with any mods or tools.
* **Set-and-Forget Automation:** Enter your preferred timer interval (e.g., 10 or 15 minutes) and click **▶ Start Auto-Backup Loop**. The app will run quietly in the background, backing up your files automatically while you play!
* **How to Restore:** If your game bugs out or resets, click **⏪ Restore Latest Save** to instantly overwrite the broken files with your last healthy backup.

---

## 🔍 Common Steam Save Locations
If you aren't sure where your game hides its saves, check these common Windows paths:
* `C:\Users\<YourUsername>\Documents\My Games\`
* `C:\Users\<YourUsername>\AppData\Local\`
* `C:\Users\<YourUsername>\Saved Games\`
* `C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\userdata\<YourSteamID>\<GameAppID>\`

---

## 🔒 Safety & Portability
SaveGuard Pro is fully open-source and packed into a standalone executable. It operates entirely locally, requires no administrative access, and never communicates with the internet. Your data stays entirely yours.
